Delete your HealthResponse account and data

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Profile → Privacy & data.
  2. Tap Deactivate my account and confirm.

Your account is deactivated, you are signed out on all devices, and push tokens are removed.

By email (permanent erasure request)

Send an email from the address registered to your account to [operator email address] with the subject "Delete my HealthResponse data". The operator will verify your identity and erase or anonymise your personal data, except records the operator is legally required to keep (for example audit logs).

What is removed

  • Profile, emergency contacts, medications and reminders, notification inbox, push tokens.
  • Records and uploaded files you created.
  • Emergency locations are removed automatically 90 days after an event ends.
