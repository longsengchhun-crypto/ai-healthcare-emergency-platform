HealthResponse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Who we are

HealthResponse is a demonstration healthcare application. The service operator is the person or organisation that hosts the backend this app connects to. Contact: [operator email address].

Data we collect

  • Account: name, email address, optional phone number, password (stored only as a hash).
  • Optional health profile: date of birth, blood group, allergies, conditions, address.
  • Emergency contacts you enter (name, phone, relationship).
  • Appointments, medical notes and files you or authorised clinicians add; medications and reminders.
  • Emergency requests: status history and, only if you allow it at that moment, the location captured.
  • Optional motion-sensor evidence for a "possible incident" check, only if you turn monitoring on.
  • A notification inbox, a push-notification token if push is enabled, and security/audit events (who accessed what, when).
  • Metadata about AI requests (type, status, duration) — not the text you send.

Why

To provide the app's features, keep the service secure, and investigate misuse. We do not sell data and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Location

Location is requested only when you send an emergency request or tap "Check location". It is never collected in the background.

Who can see your data

  • Authorised hospital staff of the receiving queue can see your name, phone and shared location for an emergency.
  • Clinicians can read your records only if you granted access or have a confirmed/completed appointment with them.
  • If the operator enables them: an SMS provider (to text your emergency contacts, only if you opt in), Google Firebase (push delivery), and an email provider (password-reset emails).

Retention

Locations of finished emergencies are removed after 90 days, sensor evidence after 30 days, and expired sessions after 30 days. Other data is kept until you deactivate your account, after which the operator removes or anonymises it according to its retention policy.

Your choices

Edit your profile, remove contacts, revoke clinician access, turn sensors and SMS alerts off, and deactivate your account in the app. See how to delete your account and data.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, sessions are short-lived, and access to sensitive data is logged. No system is perfectly secure.

Not a medical device

HealthResponse does not diagnose or treat conditions and is not a replacement for professional care or your local emergency number. AI features provide general information and can be wrong.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change.
