## Full description (max 4000)
```
HealthResponse is a healthcare companion that brings everyday care and emergency requests into one app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Find doctors and hospitals and request appointments
• Keep your own health notes and attach PDF or image files
• See notes added by your clinicians (read-only)
• Set medication reminders on your phone
• Ask the AI helper for general information (it never diagnoses)
• Send an emergency request with your location to a hospital response queue and follow its real status
• Optionally let the app text your emergency contacts when you send a real request
• Optional motion-sensor safety check that always asks "Are you OK?" first

IMPORTANT
• HealthResponse is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
• AI features give general information only and can be wrong or incomplete.
• Emergency requests go to the response queue operated by the service provider that runs this app. They do not
  replace calling your local emergency number. If you are in danger, call emergency services first.
• Features that depend on a service provider (hospital response, SMS, push notifications) work only where that
  provider has enabled them.

PRIVACY
• Location is used only when you ask for help or check it, never in the background.
• Hospital staff cannot browse your medical records.
• You can deactivate your account in the app.
```
